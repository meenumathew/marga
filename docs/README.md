# Marga Documentation

Every page here has one job, following the Diataxis model that Marga also
applies to your own notes.

## How-to: get something done

- [Create a site from the template](how-to/create-a-site-from-the-template.md):
  scaffold your own site with the CLI.
- [Configure your site](how-to/configure-your-site.md): change the name,
  wording, colours, and where your notes live.
- [Update your site](how-to/update-your-site.md): bring in later template
  improvements without losing your notes or settings.
- [Deploy](how-to/deploy.md): publish the site as a read-only reader.
- [Run the quality gates](how-to/run-quality-gates.md): the checks that run
  locally and in CI.
